## ID Task 3

#  Causality Assessment of ADR (Amoxicillin Case)

## 1. Objective

Evaluate the relationship between a drug and an adverse reaction using **standard causality assessment methods**. Analyze the case and classify the reaction as **Certain, Probable, or Possible**.

---

## 2. Case Scenario

A 28-year-old female patient was prescribed **Amoxicillin (500 mg, oral, TID)** for a respiratory tract infection. After **3 days**, she developed a **generalized itchy skin rash (maculopapular rash)**. The medication was discontinued, and symptoms resolved within 4 days.

---

## 3. Patient Details

* Age: 28 years
* Gender: Female
* Medical History: No chronic illness
* Allergy History: Not known
* Diagnosis: Respiratory tract infection

---

## 4. Drug Information

* Drug Name: Amoxicillin
* Dose: 500 mg
* Route: Oral
* Frequency: Three times daily
* Indication: Infection
* Start Date: Day 0
* Stop Date: Day 3

---

## 5. Adverse Reaction

* Type: Maculopapular skin rash
* Onset: Day 3
* Severity: Mild to Moderate
* Outcome: Recovered after drug withdrawal

---

## 6. Timeline

| Event            | Time  |
| ---------------- | ----- |
| Drug Started     | Day 0 |
| Reaction Started | Day 3 |
| Drug Stopped     | Day 3 |
| Recovery         | Day 7 |

---

## 7. Causality Assessment Criteria

### 7.1 Temporal Relationship

* Reaction occurred after drug initiation → **Strong association**

### 7.2 Known ADR

* Amoxicillin is known to cause **hypersensitivity skin reactions**

### 7.3 Dechallenge

* Reaction improved after stopping drug → **Positive**

### 7.4 Rechallenge

* Not performed due to ethical concerns

### 7.5 Alternative Causes

* No other medications or infections explaining rash

---

## 8. WHO-UMC Causality Assessment

* **Probable**

  * Reasonable time relationship
  * Known ADR
  * Positive dechallenge
  * No alternative explanation

---

## 9. Naranjo Algorithm Score

| Question                        | Score |
| ------------------------------- | ----- |
| Previous reports of reaction    | +1    |
| Event after drug given          | +2    |
| Improvement after stopping drug | +1    |
| Rechallenge not done            | 0     |
| Alternative causes ruled out    | +2    |
| **Total Score**                 | **6** |

 **Interpretation: Probable ADR**

---

## 10. Final Classification

* **Causality Category:** ✅ **Probable**
* **Suspected Drug:** Amoxicillin

---

## 11. Severity Assessment

* Mild to Moderate (non-life-threatening but clinically significant)

---

## 12. Clinical Management

* Drug discontinued ✔️
* Antihistamines given for rash ✔️
* Alternative antibiotic prescribed ✔️

---

## 13. Conclusion

This case demonstrates a **Probable Adverse Drug Reaction to Amoxicillin**, supported by:

* Clear temporal relationship
* Well-documented ADR profile
* Positive dechallenge
* Lack of alternative causes

---

## 14. Tools Used

* WHO-UMC Causality Scale
* Naranjo Algorithm
