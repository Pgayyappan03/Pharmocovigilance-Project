#  ADR Case Analysis (Adverse Drug Reaction)

##  Objective

Analyse patient case scenarios to identify potential **Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs)** by evaluating:

* Symptoms
* Medication history
* Timeline

---

## ADR Analysis Workflow

### 1. Patient Details

* Age: 45
* Gender: Male    
* Medical History: Hypertension
* Diagnosis: Essential Hypertension

---

### 2.  Medication History

|Enalapril| 5mg | Oral | Once Daily |  Day 0 | Hypertension |



---

### 3.  Symptoms

* Onset: Day 7 after Starting Medication
* Description: Persistent dry, non-productive cough
* Severity: Moderate
* Organ System: Respiratory System

---

### 4. Timeline

| Event             | Date |
| ----------------- | ---- |
| Drug Started      | Day 0|
| Symptoms Started  | Day 7|
| Drug Stopped      |Day 10|
| Symptoms Resolved |Day 14|

---

### 5.  Causality Check

* Temporal relationship  (Symptoms appeared after drug initiation)
* Known ADR  (ACE inhibitors commonly cause dry cough)
* Dechallenge (Symptoms improved after stopping drug)
* Rechallenge  (Not performed due to patient discomfort)

---

### 6. Differential Diagnosis

* Disease progression (Hypertension does not cause cough)
* Drug interactions (No other medications used)
* Other causes (No infection/allergy history)
---

### 7. Causality Assessment (WHO-UMC)

Probable

Reason: Reasonable time relationship + known ADR + positive dechallenge

---

### 8. Suspected Drug

* Drug Name: Enalapril
* Justification:
* Strong temporal association
* Well-documented ADR (ACE inhibitor-induced cough)
* Symptom resolution after withdrawal

---

### 9.  Severity

* Moderate (Affected quality of life but not life-threatening)

---

### 10.  Management

* Stop drug? Yes
* Alternative drug: Losartan (ARB – no cough side effect)
* Treatment: Symptomatic relief (cough suppression if needed)



##  Conclusion

ADR identification depends on:

* Clear temporal relationship
* Known pharmacological effect
* Positive dechallenge
